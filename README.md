VANGUARD — Command the Drive.

VANGUARD is a next-generation 3D car rental experience designed to transform the traditional process of discovering and renting a vehicle into an immersive digital journey.

Built around a bold black-and-white aesthetic, cinematic visuals, dynamic motion, and 3D-inspired interactions, VANGUARD puts the car and the driving experience at the center of the interface. Users can explore a curated fleet of 45 vehicles across leading automotive brands, discover each vehicle through dedicated details and specifications, choose their preferred rental experience, and move seamlessly toward booking.

The platform is designed not only for customers, but also around the operational challenges of car rental management—creating a foundation for vehicle availability, rental scheduling, digital contracts, customer management, and organized bookings.

Rather than feeling like a traditional rental platform, VANGUARD is designed to feel like stepping into a digital showroom—where every interaction builds anticipation for the drive ahead.

VANGUARD isn't just about renting a car.
It's about choosing what you want to feel behind the wheel.
